# M1 — Modular scene packs + real clock

**Status:** Implemented and verified on the iPad. Awaiting approval before M2.
**Scope:** `CLAUDE.md` › Current milestone and `docs/spec/LOCKED_MVP_SPEC.md` §9.
**Date:** 2026-11-14

## Decisions

- **Art came first.** The PixelLab room uses the whole 480×352 canvas, so the safe rectangle changed from M0's 384×256 to **452×328** (hypothesis, spec §2.3). Result: 5× on the 11-inch (measured), 6× on 12.9/13-inch, 5× on the 10.9-inch Air (unit-tested only).
- **Camera:** three-quarter view from behind the desk chair (`study-room-back-3q-v1`), because that is what the generated room gave. It matches the MVP board better than M0's flat side view.
- **Layered rendering (user rule, now in `CLAUDE.md`):** groups `scenery < room < actors < foreground < objects < debug`. The room's keyed-out glass is the window mask. The chair back is a `foreground` piece so it really occludes the character.
- **Characters contain no chair.** PixelLab painted each character into the room's chair. No PixelLab tool on the trial plan can separate them (`cut_out_room_furniture` only works on `create_room` rooms; `inpaint_image` needs a paid tier). So `Tools/build_art.py` does it: a hand-traced envelope cuts the figure out, a hand-traced polygon lifts the chair back and slats into `chair_front.png`, and the body is continued behind the chair with nearest-colour fill.
- **Pack format:** one folder per pack with `manifest.json` + a one-row `sheet.png`; folder reference in the bundle. Not Xcode `.spriteatlas`: each sheet is already one texture, and folders make "add a pack without code changes" literal.
- **Anchor rule:** every pack sprite sits with its bottom-left on its anchor (M0 used bottom-centre).
- **Cat sits on the window sill**, not the desk as on the board: the desk has no free space next to lamp, clock and keyboard.
- **Clock skin** is a hand-drawn 23×15 bezel with 3×5 emissive cyan digits, drawn over the room's painted clock (review D4). The colon does not blink.
- **Pack choice** is by launch argument for now; the picker UI is M2 scope.

## Open issues

1. **Hidden body parts are invented.** Behind the chair the character is flat nearest-colour fill. Invisible now; a pose that moves the torso (M2 stretch) needs properly drawn frames.
2. **Character sprites have pin-holes** where dark clothing matched the dark room within the cut-out threshold. They show the room's near-identical pixel, so nothing is visible in place, but the sprites would look wrong on a different room.
3. **Animations are minimal:** a 1 px head bob at 1 fps and a 1 px cat breath at 0.5 fps. Drink, stretch and rest are M2.
4. **Black cat is low-contrast** against the dark railing of city 1.
5. Suspend/resume of the clock is covered by simulated notifications, not by a long real suspend on the device.
6. Other iPad sizes are unit-tested only. M0's energy baseline is still not measured.
7. Day/night variants of the art (review D2) are M3.
